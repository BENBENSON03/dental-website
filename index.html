Good to know

Questions,
answered.

Will my results look natural? +

That is always the goal. We work with your existing features and use digital planning to keep results balanced, subtle and recognisably yours.

Do you offer finance? +

Yes. We offer flexible monthly payment plans for eligible treatments. We will explain the options clearly during your consultation.

How soon can I book? +

We usually have consultation appointments available within two weeks. Contact us and our patient team will find a time that works for you.

Will I see the plan before treatment? +

Always. For relevant treatments, you will see a digital preview and have time to ask questions before deciding.
